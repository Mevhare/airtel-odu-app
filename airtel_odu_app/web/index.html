    Why the numbers differ

    Device totals vs. data totals

    Devices-tab totals come from the router: WiFi traffic only, since its last restart. Data-tab totals come from the outdoor unit: everything on the mobile link since install, wired included. They measure different things over different spans, so they won't match — use device figures to compare devices, and the outdoor unit's figures for your allowance.
